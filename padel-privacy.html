Padel Americano · Privacy

Applies to the Padel Americano app for iPhone and iPad (listed on the App Store as "Americano Padel Rounds"), published by ViDesk Ltd. Last updated 12 October 2026.

Padel Americano collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. It never sends anything to us.

What stays on your device

The app saves your events on your device: event names, the player names you type in, courts, rounds and scores, plus your language choice. This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app, or event by event from the Event tab. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

When you share a round or the results, the app hands the text to the iOS share sheet. What happens next (for example sending it on WhatsApp) is between you and the app you choose.

The Pro purchase

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple tells the app only that the purchase exists so it can unlock the Pro features, and that works on every device signed in with your Apple ID (and through Family Sharing). Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you use the round timer, the app may ask permission to show a local notification when the time is up. That notification is created on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete. To remove the app's saved events, delete them in the app or delete the app.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that behaves differently.

Support and contact

Questions, a format you'd like the app to run, or something that didn't work at your club? Write to ViDesk Ltd at jorge@videsk.com.

Refunds for the Pro purchase are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. To restore Pro on a new device, open the Event tab and tap "Restore purchases".
