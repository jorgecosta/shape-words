BusGlance · Privacy

Applies to the BusGlance app for iPhone, published by ViDesk Ltd. Last updated 23 October 2026.

Your location stays on your iPhone. BusGlance collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. It never sends anything to us.

Your location

With your permission, BusGlance uses your iPhone's location while the app is open to find the bus stops around you. It looks them up in a list of stops built into the app, so your position never leaves your iPhone. The app doesn't use your location in the background and doesn't keep a record of where you've been. You can turn location off in Settings and still look at the buses at a few places in London.

Live bus times

For the stops on screen, the app asks Transport for London's public data service for the buses due, their routes and their next stops. Those requests name bus stops and buses, never your position or anything about you. Like any website you visit, TfL's servers see the request and your internet address; TfL handles that under its own privacy policy.

Maps

The map is Apple Maps, provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What stays on your iPhone

The app remembers the places you recently picked as destinations and keeps a copy of bus routes for a few days so it doesn't fetch them every time. This stays on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that behaves differently.

Support and contact
