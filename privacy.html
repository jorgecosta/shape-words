Shape Words · Privacy

Applies to the Shape Words app for iPhone and iPad, published by ViDesk Ltd. Last updated 10 October 2026.

Shape Words collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. It doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app saves two things on your device so you can carry on where you left off:

  • the puzzle you're currently playing, and
  • a count of how many phrases you've completed.

This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

About the phrases

Every phrase in the game is made of random words from a public word list. The phrases are fictional and aren't connected to any wallet or account. The app never asks you to type or share anything. Never enter a real recovery phrase into any app or website.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete. To remove the app's saved puzzle and count, delete the app.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that behaves differently.
