Tiltitude · Privacy

Applies to the Tiltitude game for iPhone, published by ViDesk Ltd. Last updated 11 October 2026.

Tiltitude collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. The only link that leaves the app is the "Privacy policy" link in Settings, which opens this page in your browser.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center on your phone, the game sends your best altitude to the Tiltitude leaderboard, where other players see it next to your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your score, name or any other Game Center data. If you'd rather not appear, sign out of Game Center in the Settings app, or turn off its leaderboard sharing there.

Sharing a score with the Share button is up to you: the game only fills in the message, and you choose where it goes.

Motion sensors

You steer by tilting your phone, so the game reads the phone's motion sensors while you play. That reading is used instantly to move the ball and is never stored or sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device:

  • your best altitude and number of runs,
  • whether you've seen "How to play" and which in-game tips you've seen, and
  • your sound, haptics and steering settings.

This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete. To remove the app's saved records, delete the app.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that behaves differently.
