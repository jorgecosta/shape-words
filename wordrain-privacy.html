WordRain · Privacy

Applies to the WordRain app for iPhone and iPad, published by ViDesk Ltd. Last updated 10 October 2026.

WordRain collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. The game never connects to the internet. The only link that leaves the app is the "Privacy policy" link in Settings, which opens this page in your browser.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device:

  • your best score, furthest wave, longest word and number of games played,
  • whether you've seen the "How to play" screen, and
  • your sound and haptics settings.

This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app (or when you choose "Reset records" in Settings). Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete. To remove the app's saved records, reset them in Settings or delete the app.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that behaves differently.
