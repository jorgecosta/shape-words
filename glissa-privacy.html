Glissa · Privacy

Applies to the Glissa game for iPhone, published by ViDesk Ltd. Last updated 17 October 2026.

Glissa collects no data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. It works fully offline. The only links that leave the app are the "Privacy" link on the Levels screen, which opens this page in your browser, and "Rate Glissa", which opens the App Store. "Share" and "Tell a friend" open your phone's own share sheet, and you choose whether and where to send anything.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things on your device:

  • which levels you've unlocked, and your stars and best score on each, and
  • your sound and haptics settings.

This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center on your phone, the game sends your total score (your best score on every level, added up) to the Glissa leaderboard, where other players see it next to your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your score, name or any other Game Center data. If you'd rather not appear, sign out of Game Center in the Settings app, or turn off its leaderboard sharing there.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there's nothing to access, correct or delete. To remove the app's saved records, delete the app.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that behaves differently.
